Services

Clear scope. Plain pricing. Work your team can keep running.

Most clients start with discovery, move to implementation, and stay on for support. If you already know what you need built, we can start there.

Business discovery

1–2 weeks · Fixed fee

We spend time with the people doing the work, map your key processes, and look at the tools you already pay for. You come out with a short, ranked plan, not a 60-page deck.

What you get

  • Interviews with your team members
  • A map of where time goes in your core workflows
  • A ranked list of opportunities with estimated hours saved and cost
  • Honest notes on what not to automate yet
  • A 60-minute readout with your leadership

Implementation

2–4 weeks per project · Scoped up front

We take the top items from discovery and help you build them. We don't just automate; we look to streamline and optimize across your business as a first step. We connect the pieces: usually your email, CRM, spreadsheets, and an AI model doing a specific job. We test it with real cases before anyone relies on it.

What you get

  • Working automations for agreed workflows (e.g. lead intake, quote drafts, invoice processing, weekly reports)
  • Simple documentation in plain English
  • A review step wherever a mistake would be costly
  • 30 days of post-launch support included

Ongoing support

Month to month · Cancel anytime

Tools change, vendors update models, and your business shifts. Ongoing support keeps what we built working and moving forward, without hiring in-house.

What you get

  • A set block of hours each month
  • Monitoring and fixes for automations we've built
  • A monthly check-in on what's working and what's next
  • Priority response when something breaks
  • Quick how-to help for your team as tools change
